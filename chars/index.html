About

U.S. Asset Characteristics Data provides documented monthly characteristic panels, portfolio-performance summaries, and replication resources for empirical asset-pricing research.

The current release covers U.S. equities and U.S. corporate bonds. Each dataset has its own construction documentation and release history, while the homepage provides comparable long–short portfolio views across the two asset classes.
